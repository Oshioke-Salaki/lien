What stops the owner taking the loan?

The business account has no keys, so nobody can withdraw from it. Borrowed money can only pay approved suppliers, up to a daily limit, and its code is frozen until the loan is repaid.

What do lenders own if a business defaults?

A business that keeps selling. After foreclosure the owner's share goes to lenders too, until someone buys the business at auction. The price pays lenders first.

Does a Lien business depend on trusting Intel?

Partly, yes. The quote proves which build ran on which hardware. It does not protect against a broken chip, and the security notes say so.

How is a Lien loan different from an order advance?

An order advance can never be bigger than the order. Lien lends against the whole business, so it can fund growth, and a default hands lenders something that still earns.

Is a Lien loan live with real money?

Not yet. It runs on NEAR testnet with test dollars, and the contracts are unaudited.
