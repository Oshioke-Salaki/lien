# Lien in two minutes, for judges

**What it is.** Lien is a market of loans to AI businesses on NEAR. Each business is a contract account with zero access keys that runs one pinned build inside Intel TDX through OutLayer, and every sale it makes pays its lenders first. If it stops paying, anyone can foreclose: the business keeps serving its customers from the same address, now earning for lenders, until someone buys it at auction.

**Live app:** [lien-five.vercel.app](https://lien-five.vercel.app) on NEAR testnet. No wallet needed: **Get a free test account** creates a funded testnet account in your browser in about ten seconds.

**Secondary market for loan shares.** A lender's share of a loan is a note, and notes are transferable: `transfer_note(loan_id, to)` in `contracts/agent/src/notes.rs` moves the whole note, with its principal and any repayments not yet collected, to another account. It needs a full-access key (1 yoctoNEAR attached), merges into a note the recipient already holds, and is locked while the note has voted on an open code-change proposal, so votes cannot be counted twice. Its value is readable on chain: `get_note` returns the principal, what was claimed and what is claimable now, and `get_loan` the amount repaid and still owed, so a buyer can price it. In the app, **Your positions** has **Sell or give your share to someone else**. Tests: `a_transferred_note_carries_its_history`, `merging_notes_keeps_the_holder_count_right`, `transferring_a_note_needs_a_full_access_key`, `self_transfer_is_refused`, `the_same_principal_cannot_vote_twice_by_moving_the_note` (`contracts/agent/src/tests.rs`), and the sandbox test `an_unregistered_recipient_cannot_block_anything_and_keeps_a_credit`. On testnet, a note on a foreclosed loan was [moved to another account](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/2ZFiDVZ729qkLDPvpUmsUYEZouGZXVDZEJjY95SXpQWy) and [back](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/DQR67ULxkYfKk1RXFHoWoYDLZZnJ5BeQ6Hbcf3XnABgZ) with its $5.23 uncollected, after a stranger's attempt to move it was refused ([recorded](docs/evidence/note-transfer-testnet.json); `cd ops && npm run note-transfer`). 

**Atomic sales through a keyless market.** `contracts/notemarket`, deployed as `notemarket.lien-13569.testnet` with zero access keys and no owner, setter or upgrade method, makes a sale one transaction instead of two trusting steps. The business contracts were not changed. The seller signs `list` (0.01 NEAR bond, returned), `transfer_note` to the market, and `confirm`; the market checks each step with the business's own `get_note`. Reserving first is what makes it safe: `transfer_note` carries no message, so the market ties the note that arrives to the seller who reserved the loan's only listing while the market held nothing on it, and `confirm` requires the market to hold exactly that principal and the seller none. The buyer sends the exact price with `ft_transfer_call(msg = {"buy": {...}})`; the market calls `transfer_note(loan_id, buyer)` and, in the callback, pays the seller only if that succeeded, or returns the full amount so the token refunds the buyer (NEP-141). A wrong token, a wrong price, an unlisted or unconfirmed note, or a sale already in flight is refused and refunded; the listing is locked during a sale or cancel, so a double buy cannot happen. A refused seller payout is kept as a credit anyone can retry. Unclaimed repayments go with the note (claim before listing to keep them). On testnet: [list](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/8WNgnPEzUCnGFvM9cbZHxhiX953ShDsAnbAfNFQtQKkh), [transfer](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/ERCQ6JprnicyKMpoVoBHG9ZVQ67XAZ8vwsAir22XTPHb), [confirm](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/Gi7xmGAGXD68VpfGvX24a85msvvyHigWKLzhG5R7rffX), [a wrong price refunded](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/6UWphjDVfuyo3Azye5yvkL1WMJDgSYgfMECwr3Ndhfg2), [the sale: note to the buyer and 30 tUSD to the seller in one transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/AuLQRYRhWgGSrieajYsK9Ve16Ax4JESTnmNuD9kCPSMJ), [a second buy refunded](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/2wmf1NSnsvqHacJiQvwPAmsM143eLHg7FCzwHvynCJJw), [a cancel returning the note](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/FR6rbtYFTG2ifzfZWDSKocF6Ej9pvc4i9Zk6uxZugBnH), and [a sale back](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/8sMUS8eFi3ZvRZABdDcJ5qkave7wEr8o3iN5GfjXJsDA) ([recorded](docs/evidence/note-market-testnet.json); `cd ops && npm run notemarket -- demo`). A buy the business itself refuses is shown in the sandbox (a vote-locked buyer, refunded in full), not on testnet. MCP: `list_notes_for_sale`, `prepare_list_note`, `prepare_buy_note`. Limits: one listing per loan at a time; only accounts directly under the registry can be listed, and the registry account has a full-access key, so that check does not by itself prove a business's code; a note sent to the market without a listing first cannot be recovered. There is no order book or app page for it yet.

## How Lien differs from the closest entries we found

These are good projects, and Incorpr is further along than Lien: it is live on mainnet, and Lien is on testnet only. What follows describes each project as we understand it, and what Lien does differently.

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| Incorpr | Live on mainnet. AI companies with keyless vaults; a company can borrow against itself from one lender, and while it is pledged the holder's signing is frozen; on default the pledge is auctioned |
| Loca | Agents get advances against paid customer orders held in escrow |
| RunWay | Revenue-based working capital for human merchants, split at a keyless settlement account |
| Float | A tranched lending pool for agents |
| Parallax | A CFO agent runs sealed-bid jobs among many agents, with a treasury and escrow contract and a revenue split. Its bank account `bank.useparallax.near` holds one full-access key (checked over mainnet RPC on 14 November 2026), so a person can still move its money |

What Lien does differently, each with its test:

- **A market of many lenders.** Up to 50 lenders fund one loan, each holding a note for their share and paid in proportion to it (`lenders_claim_in_proportion_to_their_note`, and `notes_together_never_claim_more_than_the_pool` property-tested in `math.rs`; the live run's loan had [two](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/D49E1YeRZwG9K5yffDZZvXDNbbLu4cWY6xPbsB8do6Uf) [lenders](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/76G9qjZQ46eZHW1g6QJowfqUy25nHiSgSLErB6SujaKg)).
- **Lenders are paid first from every sale.** Each payment runs the waterfall before the operator or working capital see it (`math.rs` `split_inflow`, property-tested; [job tx](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/6NqUsdMC1iCdXTLVj65RofZU6pxwHvyV5onU6XvfWFMw)).
- **Anyone can foreclose, with no keeper or admin** (`anyone_can_foreclose_after_grace_and_the_clock_needs_no_keeper`; [a stranger forecloses](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/74LFQQGHTSo9v7uLB8u6qgMR5UTqFXJMw1fgqPLN9a3g)).
- **A foreclosed business keeps earning for its lenders, then is put up for auction** (`a_foreclosed_business_keeps_earning_for_lenders`, `a_settled_sale_pays_lenders_and_hands_over_the_business`; [job after foreclosure](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/Aja18SN2rgSPrcni1i7Z62xgarwS91tMYsaaPYt6dktF), [sale settled](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/3smw9TPRdYZHNiTciP4gFMVnjRb5UYmRR1KSjF4k4nMf)).

Where others are ahead: Incorpr is on mainnet, and on Loca lenders compete on price, while Lien's terms are fixed by the borrower.

## Hire Sentinel on NEAR's Agent Market

**Live on NEAR's [Agent Market](https://market.near.ai/a/lien-sentinel)** as `lien-sentinel`, $0.25 a check, payable per call with x402 in USDC ([agent card](https://lien-sentinel.market.near.ai/.well-known/agent-card.json)). The marketplace's signed test ping reached the webhook and was verified, and an unpaid call answers `402` with the price. Try it: `curl -s -X POST https://lien-sentinel.market.near.ai/a2a/v1 -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"SendMessage","params":{"message":{"role":"ROLE_USER","messageId":"1","parts":[{"text":"check outlayer.near"}]}}}'`

Sentinel is built to work as a hired agent on [market.near.ai](https://market.near.ai). A `hire.created` webhook whose brief names an account (`alice.near` for mainnet, `bob.testnet` for testnet) reaches `POST /api/agent-market/webhook`; the signature is checked, the same rules as Sentinel's pinned build are run by Lien's server, and the report (score, ALLOW / WARN / BLOCK, flags, facts, a link to buy the attested version) is submitted through the marketplace's `start` and `submit`. A brief without an account is declined and refunded. `GET /api/agent-market/poll` serves any assignment a webhook missed. The checks run on Lien's server, not in the TEE; the attested check is the one bought [on Lien](https://lien-five.vercel.app/b/sentinel.v1.lien-13569.testnet). ## Honest limits

- **Testnet only, and unaudited.** The mainnet path is documented in [docs/DEPLOYMENT.md](docs/DEPLOYMENT.md); it is not deployed.
- **An operator can run a copy elsewhere.** They lose the account, inbox, record and sealed credentials, so loans are only safe up to what leaving forfeits.
- **Revenue that never touches the business is invisible.** Lien secures what flows through the account and its inbox, nothing else.
- **We trust OutLayer and Intel TDX** for results, but a forged result still cannot pay anyone outside the vendor policy or reach what lenders are owed.
- **No real borrowers yet.** Sentinel is our own example agent, and its customers on testnet are our test accounts.
- **NEAR Intents: one settlement through NEAR's verifier on testnet; 1Click is still quote-only.** One 1 tUSD Intents payment settled into Sentinel's inbox through NEAR's unmodified verifier contract, which Lien deployed to testnet itself because Intents has no official testnet deployment; it is NEAR's code (same hash as `intents.near`) but not an instance NEAR runs. No solver, market maker or 1Click took part, and nothing was swapped from another chain: the payer deposited test tokens and signed a withdrawal to the inbox. The app's 1Click flow is unchanged: it asks the mainnet 1Click API for a real quote, deposit address and live status, with sending switched off. Mainnet 1Click refuses a `.testnet` account as a NEAR destination, so on testnet the quote credits the business's inbox name inside the Intents ledger (`recipientType: INTENTS`) instead of delivering to it on NEAR (`DESTINATION_CHAIN`, which the app uses on mainnet); money sent to a testnet quote's address could never reach the business, which is why the panel says not to send. `KNOWN_DEPOSIT_TX` through `SUCCESS`, `REFUNDED` and `FAILED` have only been shown in the dry run and the unit tests, not from a live 1Click deposit.
- **Chain Signatures is a narrow vault.** `contracts/xvault` is a keyless contract per business that controls one EVM address and signs only a native-ETH (or one ERC-20) transfer on Base Sepolia to one settlement address fixed at init; anything else is refused before the MPC signer is asked. On testnet the vault was created with no key, a transfer to another address was refused, and the MPC network signed a transfer that recovers to the vault's address ([evidence](docs/evidence/testnet-e2e.md#chain-signatures-revenue-on-base-sepolia-that-can-only-go-one-way)). That transfer [landed on Base Sepolia](https://sepolia.basescan.org/tx/0x15b1b31a4cf9591b486d0c60806fb263588e8286bd9796bfebd2481b92780bd0) (block 58613924). The settlement address is held by the Lien team and stands in for a route into the business's inbox; nothing moves money from it into the waterfall, and the vault does not count toward a loan on its own. The vault is separate from the business contract, so the agent's code cannot reach it.
- **The lending vault bounds a stolen agent key; it does not pick borrowers.** `contracts/lendvault` refuses anything outside its fixed policy, but within it the agent chooses the business, and anyone can list a business through the registry, so `max_per_loan`, `max_share_bps` and `total_budget` are what limit a compromised agent. The vault trusts the registry's listing (the registry account holds a key) and cannot read a business's access keys itself; it relies on the registry creating every business with none. `total_budget` counts principal ever accepted, so repayments do not refill it. The MCP lender (`lien-lender-645368.testnet`) still holds a full key, to hire Sentinel and to lend directly.

Every limit has its reasoning in [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md) and [docs/FAQ.md](docs/FAQ.md).
